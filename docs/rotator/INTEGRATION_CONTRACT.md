# Later semantic integration contract

The final Sweep 2 integration may bind these stable package-local ports: `RotatorOperatingContextPort`, `RotatorTargetSourcePort`, `RotatorReviewPort`, `RotatorRadioStatePort`, `RotatorSatellitePort`, `RotatorReadOnlyPort`, `RotatorActionPort`, `RotatorSerialTransportPort`, and `RotatorHamlibPort`.

Central integration must preserve the existing USB enumeration/permission owner and operating-context authority; add Rotator navigation/settings/status without duplicating controllers; bind exact selections from Band Maps, DX, Neural, DX Chaser, Contest, Portable/Operations and Satellite Operations; extend configuration, privacy, support and System Health semantically; and keep the active automation arm, tracking session, current target, motion, pending commands and raw identifiers out of backup.

Allowed target intents are explicit manual/grid/DX-detail/Band-Map/Neural/DX-Chaser/Contest/Portable/Operations/satellite/QO-100 selections. Incoming cluster, PSK/RBN/WSPR rows, provider refresh, app launch, import, recomposition, Groups.io and unselected N1MM packets cannot move hardware.

The integration must physically test the owner-present ARCO read-only before any manual movement and must not infer hardware, UI, service, RF or TX success from this branch’s source/build results.
