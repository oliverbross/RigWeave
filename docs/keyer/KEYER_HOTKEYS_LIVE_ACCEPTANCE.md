# Keyer hotkeys v2 — live acceptance checklist

This checklist is **not executed** by the source task. It requires an operator-controlled device/radio session and an authorised RF-safe environment.

- Confirm all six legacy CW messages and six voice slots survive update.
- Confirm hotkeys default off and no key is intercepted while off.
- Verify delivered F1–F12/modifier chords, repeat suppression, unbound propagation, capture, clear, and conflict swap/cancel.
- Focus callsign/exchange/search/settings editors and verify no hotkey dispatch.
- Verify strip layout and TalkBack labels in tablet landscape/portrait and compact phone layouts.
- Confirm profile switching and configuration restoration never arm or transmit.
- Under RF-safe controls, verify 24-character CW, rejection at 25, and Stop/Force RX.
- Preview a multi-slot plan on the tablet route and confirm no PTT.
- Under RF-safe controls, verify complete-plan preflight, one PTT, order/silence, cancel/route-loss cleanup, confirmed RX, and no PTT when a later clip is bad.
- Verify Repeat CQ explicit start, countdown/caps, busy skip, operator-input stop, Escape/background/disconnect/mode/context/profile/route stops, and no process-death resume.
- Confirm recovery contains logical settings but no WAV/path, queue, resolved call/exchange, CAT transcript, arming, or repeat runtime.

Record device/Android, app commit, radio/firmware, audio interface, keyboard, RF controls, and each observed result. A build is not physical audio, CAT, PTT, RF, or on-air evidence.
