# Android SDR Workbench v4 Live Acceptance

Use this checklist only on an approved unlocked device and an explicitly authorized receive source. Source/build, hosted, installation/process, visual, audio/control/RF and release evidence must be recorded separately.

- Verify the device identity, app UID, signing certificate, private-data/schema/QSO counts and fresh private backup before `adb install -r`.
- Confirm 180-second survival, relaunch and an empty crash buffer without uninstalling or clearing app data.
- With Debug Lab, capture recording, replay/timeline, historical waterfall, Marker A/B, inspector, tracker, Survey heatmap, scanner intelligence, four monitors, calibration, Settings and Health. Every image must show `DEMO · NO RADIO`.
- With authorized live I/Q, verify explicit recording, atomic completed files, cap behavior, offline replay, seek, 1× audio truth, non-1× audio disabled, live-frame isolation and explicit return-live attachment.
- Verify two local receivers only within the proven I/Q span; local follow never changes the physical VFO.
- Verify survey persistence/retention and historical labels; no raw I/Q/audio/decoded text/RadioText/notes/private paths appear in support output.
- Exercise TCI disconnect/reconnect, stale frames, duplicate status, capability/sample-rate change and explicit stream reattachment. Do not test PTT/TUNE/TX under this receive-only checklist.

Debug/build/package evidence cannot establish live RF, audio quality, calibrated dBm, authenticated service, CAT/PTT/TUNE, signing or publication. Record unavailable equipment or locked-screen conditions as pending, never as passed.
